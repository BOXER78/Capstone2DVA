# Student Mental Health & Burnout Analysis
**Newton School of Technology**

## 1. The Challenge
- Academic burnout is a leading cause of dropout and mental health crises in universities.
- What drives burnout: Study hours, financial stress, or academic pressure?

## 2. Our Approach
- **Data:** Massive dataset of 150,001 students.
- **Process:** ETL pipeline -> EDA -> Statistical Testing -> Tableau Dashboard.

## 3. Key Findings (The "What")
- **Pressure = Anxiety:** High academic pressure directly raises anxiety scores.
- **Sleep Quality Matters:** Poor sleep is a massive indicator of high burnout levels.
- **The Study Trap:** Higher daily study hours do not always improve CGPA, but they do increase burnout risk.

## 4. Statistical Proof (The "Why")
- Pearson correlation tests confirmed a statistically significant relationship between academic pressure and anxiety.
- T-tests analyzed gender disparities in sleep and stress.

## 5. Business Recommendations (The "Action")
1. **Re-evaluate Course Loads** to manage academic pressure.
2. **Expand Financial Aid** to reduce compounding stress.
3. **Proactive Counseling:** Target interventions at students showing early signs of sleep deprivation.
